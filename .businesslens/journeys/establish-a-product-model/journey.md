# Establish a Product Model

## Goal

A repository that has none arrives at a Product Model its team is willing to
trust and review, whichever of the three starting points it came from.

## Success criterion

`.businesslens/` exists, passes its structural check, states its own coverage
honestly, and was approved by the Developer rather than written on their behalf.
