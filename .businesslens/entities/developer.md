# Developer

A person working in a repository who decides what the product should do and
whether the recorded model still says it. They run the terminal command, invoke
the BusinessLens skills through their coding agent, approve or reject every
proposed change to product meaning, read the model back as a local report, and
review the resulting diff like any other change in the repository. The Product
keeps nothing about them: it holds no account, and every write of product
meaning is theirs by approval rather than by identity.
