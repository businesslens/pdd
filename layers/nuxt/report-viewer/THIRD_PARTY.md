# Diagram design and routing

The layout and typography of the named readings are informed by Cathryn Lavery’s
Diagram Design, reviewed at commit 2724fd2efd8c6737f6fa704fbf5da52d67375497.
Structured readings use Vue and HTML with BusinessLens identity. Graphs use
Vue Flow's MIT-licensed canvas, with BusinessLens nodes and routed SVG edges.
Vue Flow source and license: https://github.com/bcakmakoglu/vue-flow
Its MIT notice is included in licenses/vue-flow-MIT.txt.
Source: https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design
The MIT notice is included in licenses/diagram-design-MIT.txt.
