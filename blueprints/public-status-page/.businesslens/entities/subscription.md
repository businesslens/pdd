# Subscription

An email address that asked to receive the page's updates.

## Information kept

- **Email address** — where updates are sent
- **Subscribed at** — when the address was entered

## States

### Pending

Entered but not yet confirmed from the address itself. It receives nothing but
the confirmation link.

### Confirmed

Confirmed from the address. It receives every incident update and maintenance
announcement until it is unsubscribed.
