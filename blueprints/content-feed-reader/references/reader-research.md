# Reader research notes

These are product assumptions to validate with readers, not claims that research
has already proved them.

- A large unread count becomes discouraging unless readers can make bulk
  progress without unfollowing a useful source.
- Readers often remember words or a source before they remember whether they
  saved an item.
- Source failures should be visible without making the library feel damaged or
  temporarily empty.
- Saving, tagging, and collecting are distinct commitments: keeping an item,
  naming why it matters, and arranging it for an audience.
- A shared collection should be understandable before account creation, and the
  return path after sign-in should preserve the collection that prompted it.

The Product Model turns these assumptions into explicit behavior that can be
reviewed. Future research may change the model; this file does not override it.
