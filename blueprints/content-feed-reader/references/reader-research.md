# Reader assumptions

These are assumptions to validate, not claims that research has proved them.

- A growing unread count feels manageable when a Reader can make progress one
  item or one source at a time.
- Saving an item and placing it in a collection are separate decisions.
- A collection owner expects a public link to reveal only that collection.
- Unlisting must revoke the public link without deleting the private collection.

Future research may change the Product Model; this file does not override it.
