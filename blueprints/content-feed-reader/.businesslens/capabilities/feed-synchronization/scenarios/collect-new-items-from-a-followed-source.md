# Collect new items from a followed source

## Trigger

The Reader refreshes their followed sources.

## Steps

1. The Product reads each followed feed
2. Items the Reader's library does not already hold are collected
3. The newly collected items enter the Reader's unread backlog

## Outcome

New feed items are available in the Reader's private library without duplicates.
