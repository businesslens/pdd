# A reader finds an item from a half-remembered phrase

## Trigger

A reader searches their library for words they remember from an item.

## Steps

1. The product searches titles and body text across the reader's whole library, saved and unsaved alike
2. Matches are shown with their source, date, and the matching text in context
3. The reader recognises the item and opens it

## Outcome

The reader has the item in front of them, without having needed to remember its
source, its title, or when it arrived.

## Edge cases

- Items whose source has since been unfollowed are still found if the reader saved them
