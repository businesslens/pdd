# A search that matches nothing says so usefully

## Trigger

A reader searches for words that appear nowhere in their library.

## Steps

1. The product finds no matches
2. It says plainly that nothing matched, and what was searched
3. Any narrowing currently applied is called out, with a way to clear it and search the whole library

## Outcome

The reader can tell the difference between "you do not have this" and "your
filters hid it", which is the only thing that makes an empty result actionable.
