# An unlisted collection stops serving its old link

## Trigger

Someone opens the public address of a collection that has since been unlisted or
deleted.

## Steps

1. The product finds no published collection at the address
2. It responds that the collection is not available, without saying whether it ever existed, who owned it, or what it was called
3. The visitor is offered the product's own entry point rather than a dead end

## Outcome

The link is dead and reveals nothing. Someone who was never given the link learns
nothing from probing addresses, and someone who had it learns only that it is
gone.
