# Reflections

The optional weekly look back: whether the Owner wants one, the reflections
the Product prepares when a week ends, and the schedule adjustments they
suggest.

## Boundary

Owns weekly reflections, suggested adjustments and the Owner's choice to
receive them. It does not own habits: an accepted adjustment changes a habit's
schedule under the same rules as any other change the Owner makes.
