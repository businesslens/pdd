# Weekly reflection

A look back at one week of the Owner's habits, prepared by the Product when the
week ends. It proposes at most one suggested adjustment.

## Information kept

- **Week** — the calendar week it looks back on
- **Consistency** — for each active habit, how many of its scheduled days, or how much of its weekly target, were done
- **Summary** — a few sentences reading the week back, written with a language model; absent when it could not be written
