# Habit assumptions

These are assumptions to validate, not claims that research has proved them.

- A streak that ignores days a habit was never meant to happen feels fair, and
  one that a pause does not break keeps people coming back after a break.
- Being able to check off a day from the past week covers forgetting to record
  it, without making history feel editable at will.
- People who keep a habit "three times a week" think of it in weeks, so its
  streak should count weeks whose target was met rather than days.
- Pausing is the option people want when they would otherwise delete a habit
  and lose its history.
- A short weekly look back is welcome when it is optional, says what it read,
  and proposes at most one change the person must accept.
- Lowering an over-ambitious schedule helps people keep a habit more than an
  unbroken record of misses does.

Future research may change the Product Model; this file does not override it.
