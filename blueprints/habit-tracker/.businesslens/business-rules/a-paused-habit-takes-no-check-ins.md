# A paused habit takes no check-ins

While a habit is paused it is not due, Today does not list it, and no day can
be checked off for it — including days of the past week before it was paused.
Resuming it opens it to check-ins again from that day.

## Rationale

A pause is a clean break: the history shows paused days as paused, never as a
mixture of done and skipped.
