# A suggested adjustment changes nothing until accepted

While a suggested adjustment is proposed, and after it is dismissed or
outdated, its habit keeps exactly the schedule the Owner gave it. Only
accepting it gives the habit the proposed schedule, from that day on.

## Rationale

The language model that drafts a suggestion can misread the week; keeping it a
draft until the Owner accepts it is what makes it safe to offer.
