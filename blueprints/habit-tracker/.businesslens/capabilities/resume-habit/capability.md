# Habit resumption

Brings a paused habit back on the schedule it had. It is due again from its
next scheduled day, and its streak carries on from where the pause left it.

## Intent

Make coming back after a break as easy as leaving: nothing has to be set up
again, and nothing done before the pause is lost.
