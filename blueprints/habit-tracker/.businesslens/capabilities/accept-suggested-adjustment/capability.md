# Suggested adjustment acceptance

Lets the Owner accept a weekly reflection's suggested adjustment, which gives
that habit the proposed schedule from that day and opens the habit. A
suggestion for a habit the Owner has changed, paused or deleted since is
outdated and cannot be accepted.

## Intent

Keep the Owner the only one who decides a habit's schedule: a suggestion is an
offer, and accepting it is an ordinary schedule change the Owner makes.
