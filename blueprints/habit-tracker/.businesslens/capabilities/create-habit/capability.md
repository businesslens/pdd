# Habit creation

Defines a new habit with a name and a schedule: every day, on chosen weekdays,
or a number of times each week. A new habit counts from the day it is saved.

## Intent

Let the Owner say plainly how often they mean to do something, so that every
streak and reflection measures them against their own intention.
