# Habits

The Owner's habits and what they build up: defining them, changing and pausing
them, deleting them, and reading the streaks and history their check-ins add
up to.

## Boundary

Owns habits, their schedules, whether they are paused, and the streaks read
from their check-ins. It does not own weekly reflections or the adjustments
they suggest, and checking a habit off is reached from Today as well as from
the habit itself.
