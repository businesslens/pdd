# Question

One question of a quiz, scored on its own: multiple choice, true/false or short
answer.

## Information kept

- **Prompt** — what the question asks
- **Format** — multiple choice, true/false or short answer
- **Answer options** — what a learner chooses from; a short-answer question offers none and is typed instead
- **Correct answer** — the answer or answers that score: a choice, true or false, or the accepted typed answers
- **Explanation** — the feedback a learner is given for this question after submitting
- **Points** — what a correct answer is worth

## States

### Drafted

Proposed by the Quiz assistant and waiting for the creator. It is not part of
the quiz, and no learner sees it.

### Included

Part of the quiz, in its question order, and asked of every learner who takes
it.
