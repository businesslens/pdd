# Quiz assistant

The Product's AI assistant. When a creator asks, it reads the source material
they gave and drafts questions for them to approve; when a learner asks, it
assembles a practice round from the questions they missed. It chooses what to
ask and in what order, but it only proposes: it never adds a question to a
quiz, shares one, or changes a score.
