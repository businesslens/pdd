# Attempt

One learner's scored attempt at a quiz — their answers, and the score they
received.

## Information kept

- **Answers** — the answer given to each question and whether it scored
- **Score** — the points scored out of the points the quiz offers
- **Submitted at** — when the learner submitted it, or the quiz closed on it

## States

### In progress

Started and not yet submitted. The learner can leave and come back to it while
the quiz is open.

### Submitted

Scored and final for the learner. Only the creator can change a grade it
received.
