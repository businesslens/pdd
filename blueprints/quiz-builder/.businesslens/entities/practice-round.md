# Practice round

A short set of questions a learner missed, assembled by the Quiz assistant for
them to try again. It is the learner's own and never counts toward a score.

## Information kept

- **Questions** — the missed questions it repeats, in the order the Quiz assistant chose
- **Answers** — what the learner answered this time and whether each was right
- **Correct count** — how many questions the learner got right in this round

## States

### In progress

Assembled and being answered.

### Finished

Every question has been answered. Its answers inform the next practice round.
