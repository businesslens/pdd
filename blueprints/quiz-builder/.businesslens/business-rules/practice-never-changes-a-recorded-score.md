# Practice never changes a recorded score

However a learner does in practice, the score of their submitted attempt stays
what it was, and a quiz's results are drawn from submitted attempts alone.

## Rationale

Practice exists to help a learner improve. If it moved the recorded score,
results would measure persistence rather than what was understood when the
quiz was taken.
