# A proposed question changes nothing until its creator accepts it

A question the Product proposes is kept beside the quiz, not in it. It is never
asked in an attempt, never counted in a score or a result, and never repeated
in practice until the Creator accepts it.

## Rationale

Keeping drafts out of the quiz until the Creator accepts them makes every draft
a proposal, never a publication.
