# Class deletion

Deletes a class the Creator owns for good, once the Creator confirms, with who
has joined it. Its join code stops working; the quizzes assigned to it, and
every attempt made at them, are unchanged.

## Intent

Let the Creator get rid of a group they no longer teach without losing the
quizzes or the results it was used for.
