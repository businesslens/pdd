# Draft questions

Has the Quiz assistant draft questions for the Creator's quiz from source
material the Creator gives it. The drafts wait beside the quiz, each with a
correct answer and an explanation, for the Creator to add, edit or discard; none
is asked of learners until the Creator adds it.

## Intent

Save the Creator from writing every question from scratch without handing over
authorship: the assistant proposes, and the Creator decides what learners see.
