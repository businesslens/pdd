# Quiz deletion

Deletes the Creator's quiz for good, once the Creator confirms, with its questions, the
attempts learners made at it and their practice rounds. Its share link stops
working and it leaves every class it was assigned to.

## Intent

Let the Creator get rid of a quiz they no longer want, knowing it takes its
results with it.
