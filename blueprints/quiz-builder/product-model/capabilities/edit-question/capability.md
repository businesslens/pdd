# Question editing

Changes the wording, options or accepted answers, explanation or points of a
question in the Creator's quiz, or of a proposed question before they accept it.
A question keeps its format and its place in the order.

## Intent

Let the Creator fix or improve a question without starting it again, and
without disturbing scores already given.
