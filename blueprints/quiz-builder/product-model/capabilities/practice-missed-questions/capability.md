# Missed-question practice

Assembles a practice round from the questions a learner missed in a submitted
attempt — and, after an earlier round, from those still missed, the ones missed
in the most rounds first — and lets the learner answer it with feedback after
each question. A practice round never changes a score.

## Intent

Turn a learner's mistakes into their next practice, adapting to what they still
get wrong, without touching the result their Creator sees.
