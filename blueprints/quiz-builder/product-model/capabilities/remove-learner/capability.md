# Learner removal

Takes a learner out of a class the Creator owns, once the Creator confirms. The
class's quizzes are no longer listed for them through it; the attempts they made
and their practice stay theirs, with their scores.

## Intent

Let the Creator keep a class to the people it is for, such as someone who joined
by mistake or has left the group.
