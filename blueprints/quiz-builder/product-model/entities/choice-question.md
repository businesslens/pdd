# Choice question

A question of a quiz that a learner answers by choosing one of its options:
multiple choice, or true/false.

## Information kept

- **Prompt** — what the question asks
- **Format** — multiple choice or true/false
- **Answer options** — what a learner chooses from: two or more the Creator writes, or True and False
- **Correct answer** — the one option that scores
- **Explanation** — the feedback a learner is given for this question after submitting
- **Points** — what a correct answer is worth

## States

### Proposed

Drafted by the Product from source material and waiting for the Creator to
accept or dismiss it. It is not part of the quiz, and no learner sees it.

### Included

Part of the quiz, in its question order, and asked of every learner who takes
it.
