# Classes

The rosters a creator shares quizzes with: creating, renaming and deleting a
class, letting learners join it with its code, and removing a learner from it.

## Boundary

Owns a class, its join code, who has joined it and which quizzes are assigned
to it. It does not own the quizzes themselves or anything a learner answers.
