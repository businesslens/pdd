# Event RSVP assumptions

These are assumptions to validate, not claims that research has proved them.

- Guests answer more readily when no account is needed, and a name and email
  are enough for a host to recognize who is coming.
- A personal link in the confirmation email is enough for guests to find and
  change their answer later.
- Hosts expect capacity to count plus-ones, and expect a maybe to hold no spot.
- People on a waitlist accept an automatic order by when they answered, with a
  later party moving ahead only when the earlier one does not fit.
- Guests want to hear about a change of time or place, and do not want an email
  for every small edit to the description.
- Keeping an online event's link behind an answer is worth the extra step for
  guests.
- Hosts welcome suggested wording for a message only if they can edit it and
  nothing is ever sent without them.
- Hosts occasionally need to remove an answer that should not be there — a
  duplicate, a joke, someone they did not invite — and accept that the person
  is told by email and could answer again through the invitation link.

Future research may change the Product Model; this file does not override it.
