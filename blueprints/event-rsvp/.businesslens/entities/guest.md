# Guest

A person who answers an event's invitation without an account. A Guest belongs
to one event and is known by the name and email given with the answer; the
personal link emailed to them is how they come back to it.

## Information kept

- **Name** — the name they gave with their answer
- **Email** — where their confirmation, their personal link and the host's messages reach them
- **Plus-one** — whether they bring one more person
- **Responded at** — when they last gave or changed their answer, which sets their place on the waitlist

## States

### Going

Coming, and holding a spot for themselves and their plus-one.

### Maybe

Not sure yet. Holds no spot.

### Not going

Declined. Holds no spot, and stays on the guest list so the host knows.

### Waitlisted

Said yes while the event was full, and waits for a spot in the order guests
answered.
