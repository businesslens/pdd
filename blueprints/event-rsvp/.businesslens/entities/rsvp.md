# RSVP

One Guest's answer to one event's invitation. An email gives at most one RSVP
per event, and its Guest changes it through its personal link until the event
starts.

## Information kept

- **Name** — the name the Guest gave with it
- **Email** — where the confirmation, the personal link and the host's messages reach the Guest
- **Plus-one** — whether the Guest brings one more person
- **Responded at** — when the Guest last gave or changed it, which sets its place on the waitlist
- **Personal link** — the private address where its Guest sees and changes it

## States

### Going

The Guest is coming, and holds a spot for themselves and their plus-one.

### Maybe

The Guest is not sure yet. Holds no spot.

### Not going

The Guest declined. Holds no spot, and stays on the guest list so the host
knows.

### Waitlisted

The Guest said yes while the event was full, and waits for a spot in the order
RSVPs were given.
