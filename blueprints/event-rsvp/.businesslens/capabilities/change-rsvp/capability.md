# RSVP change

Lets a Guest, through the personal link to their answer, change it to going,
maybe or not going, or add or drop their plus-one, until the event starts. A
Guest who stops going frees their spots for the waitlist.

## Intent

Keep the Host's guest list true as plans change, without the Guest needing an
account or the Host having to chase anyone.
