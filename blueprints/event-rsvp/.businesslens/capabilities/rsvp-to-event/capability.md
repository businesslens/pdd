# RSVP

Records a person's answer to an invitation — going, maybe or not going — with
their name, their email and, where the Host allows it, a plus-one, without an
account. A yes that does not fit joins the waitlist. The Product emails the
Guest a personal link to their answer.

## Intent

Make answering take one visit and no account, while every answer reaches the
Host's guest list and the Guest can come back to it.
