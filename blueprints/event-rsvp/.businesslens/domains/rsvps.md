# RSVPs

The public invitation and the RSVPs given through it: opening the invitation,
answering going, maybe or not going and changing that answer, the host
removing an RSVP, and the waitlist that hands out spots as they open.

## Boundary

Owns RSVPs, plus-ones and the waitlist order. It does not own the event's
details or capacity, which only its host sets, or messages to guests.
