# RSVPs

The public invitation and the answers given through it: opening the invitation,
answering going, maybe or not going, changing that answer, and the waitlist
that hands out spots as they open.

## Boundary

Owns guests' answers, plus-ones and the waitlist order. It does not own the
event's details or capacity, which only its host sets, or messages to guests.
