# RSVPs

The public invitation and the RSVPs given through it: opening the invitation,
and answering going, maybe or not going and changing that answer.

## Boundary

Owns RSVPs, plus-ones and the waitlist order. It does not own the event's
details or capacity, which only its host sets, the host removing an RSVP, or
messages to guests.
