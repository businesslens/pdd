# Guest messaging

Sends an email from a Host to the guests of their event, chosen by their
answer, and keeps it on the event. When the Host asks for suggested wording,
the Product calls a language model with the event's details and puts the
returned subject and body in the Host's unsent message, where the Host edits
it like anything they typed.

## Intent

Give a Host one place to reach the people who are coming — or might — with
what they need to know, in words the Host has approved.
