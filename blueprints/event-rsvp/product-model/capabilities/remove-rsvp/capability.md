# RSVP removal

Lets a Host remove one RSVP from their event's guest list for good, once they
confirm, and tells its Guest by email. Spots it held open for the waitlist.

## Intent

Let a Host clear an answer that should not be there — a duplicate, a joke, a
person they did not mean to invite — without being able to change what anyone
said.
