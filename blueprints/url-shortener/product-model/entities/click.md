# Click

One follow of an active short address, kept so its Owner can see how the link
travels. A click describes where the follow came from, never who made it.

## Information kept

- **Clicked at** — when the short address was followed
- **Referring site** — the site the Visitor came from, when their browser said so
- **Country** — the country the follow came from
- **Device** — the kind of device it came from: desktop, phone or tablet
