# Link

A short address and the destination it sends Visitors to. The short address
is the Product's short domain followed by the link's slug.

## Information kept

- **Slug** — the short part of the address that names this link, generated by the Product or chosen by the Owner
- **Destination** — the full web address a Visitor is sent to
- **Expires at** — when the link stops redirecting on its own, if the Owner set an expiry
- **Created at** — when the link was created

## States

### Active

Following the short address sends a Visitor to the destination and records a
click.

### Disabled

Switched off by its Owner. The short address shows that the link is
unavailable, and nothing about the link is lost until the Owner enables it
again.

### Expired

Its expiry passed while it was active. The short address shows that the link
is unavailable until the Owner gives it a later expiry or removes it.
