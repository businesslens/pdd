# Clicks never identify a Visitor

A click keeps when it happened and the referring site, country and device it
came from — nothing that names, tracks or tells apart the person who followed
the link. Two clicks are never linked as coming from the same Visitor.

## Rationale

A Visitor follows a short address without an account and without agreeing to
be followed. Owners learn how a link travels; the Product does not learn who
followed it.
