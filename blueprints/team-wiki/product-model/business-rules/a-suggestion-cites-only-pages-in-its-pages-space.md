# A suggestion cites only pages in its page's space

Every page a suggestion cites is in the same space as the page it would update,
so every Editor who reads the suggestion may read what it relies on.

## Rationale

An agent connected by one Member must never become a way around a space's
membership, for that Member or for the Editors who read its suggestions.
