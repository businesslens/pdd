# A suggestion changes nothing until an Editor accepts it

While a suggestion is Proposed, and after it is Dismissed, its page says what it
said and every Member reads it as it was. Only accepting it publishes its
proposed content, as a revision naming the Editor who accepted it.

## Rationale

An agent can be wrong with confidence. Keeping a person between its proposal
and the page makes every change to the wiki one somebody chose.
