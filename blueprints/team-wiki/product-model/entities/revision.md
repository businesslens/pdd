# Revision

A page as it stood at one save. Every save of a page's title or content adds a
revision, and a revision is never changed. A page's revisions are removed only
with the page, when an Editor deletes it.

## Information kept

- **Title** — the page's title at that save
- **Content** — the page's content at that save
- **Saved at** — when it was saved
- **Origin** — how it came about: written by an Editor, restored from an earlier revision, or accepted from a suggestion an AI agent left
