# Pages

A space's Pages: the tree of pages a space opens to, and the page and revision
behind each entry, where Editors add, edit, arrange, delete and restore pages
and every saved revision is kept.

## Boundary

Owns page content, page placement and revision history. It does not own
reading a page, which Members reach from a space's Pages and from Search alike;
who belongs to a space; or AI agents' suggestions until an Editor accepts one as
a revision.
