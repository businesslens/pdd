# Pages

The pages of a space and their history: creating, reading, editing and
arranging pages in the space's tree, and keeping every saved revision so an
earlier one can be read or restored.

## Boundary

Owns page content, page placement and revision history. It does not own who
belongs to a space, and it does not own the Assistant's suggestions until an
editor publishes one as a revision.
