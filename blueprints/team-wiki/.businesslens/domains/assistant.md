# Assistant

What a Member's AI agent does with the wiki: answering the Member's questions
from the pages they may read, and suggesting updates to stale or conflicting
pages for Editors to publish or dismiss.

## Boundary

Owns what AI agents are given to answer from, and their suggestions and their review. It does not own
page content: a page changes only through an editor, and published suggestions
become ordinary revisions in Pages.
