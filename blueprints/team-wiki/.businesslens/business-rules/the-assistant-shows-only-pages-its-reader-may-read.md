# The Assistant shows only pages its reader may read

An answer draws on and cites only pages the Member asking may read. A
suggestion cites only pages in the same space as the page it would update,
which its Editors may read. A page the reader may not read changes nothing the
Assistant says to them.

## Rationale

The Assistant reads more than any one Member may; without this bound, asking it
would be a way around a space's membership.
