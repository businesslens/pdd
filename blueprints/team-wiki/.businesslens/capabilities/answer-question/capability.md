# Question answering

Gives an AI agent that a Member connected the pages that bear on a question the
Member asked it, from the spaces that Member belongs to, so the agent can answer
with a citation to every page it relies on. When no page the Member may read
answers it, the agent is told so and has nothing to cite.

## Intent

Let a Member get an answer in their own words while keeping the pages, not the
agent, as the authority: every claim leads back to a page the Member can open
and check.
