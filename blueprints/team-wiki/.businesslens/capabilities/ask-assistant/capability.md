# Assistant questions

Answers a Member's question in a few sentences from the pages that Member may
read, citing every page the answer relies on. When the pages hold no answer, it
says so instead of guessing.

## Intent

Let a Member get an answer in their own words while keeping the pages, not the
Assistant, as the authority: every claim leads back to a page they can open and
check.
