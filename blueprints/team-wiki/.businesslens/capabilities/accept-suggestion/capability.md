# Suggestion acceptance

An Editor of the page's space accepts a suggestion a Member's AI agent left, as
drafted or after adjusting it. Accepting publishes the update: its proposed
content becomes the page's current revision under the Editor's name.

## Intent

Make the Editor, not the agent, the one who changes a page, while sparing them
the drafting.
