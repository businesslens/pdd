# Suggestion publishing

An Editor of the page's space publishes a suggestion left by a Member's AI
agent, as drafted or after adjusting it, and its proposed content becomes the
page's current revision under the Editor's name.

## Intent

Make the Editor, not the agent, the one who changes a page, while sparing them
the drafting.
