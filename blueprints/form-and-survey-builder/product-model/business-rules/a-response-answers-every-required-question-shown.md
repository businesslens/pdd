# A response answers every required question shown

A response is received only when it answers every required question shown to
its Respondent. A question hidden by its show condition is never required.

## Rationale

Marking a question required is the Creator's promise to themselves that every
response will answer it; a Respondent is never held to a question they were
not asked.
