# A proposed question changes nothing until accepted

A proposed question is no part of its form: it has no place in the question
order, no show condition names it, and no Respondent sees it, until its
Creator accepts it.

## Rationale

Drafting must save the Creator time without ever putting a question in front
of Respondents that the Creator did not choose.
