# Anyone holding a public link responds

A response is submitted by whoever opens a form's public link, without an
account. Nothing about them is asked or kept beyond their answers.

## Rationale

Answering is meant to be as quick as opening a link; the link itself is the
Creator's way of choosing who is asked.
