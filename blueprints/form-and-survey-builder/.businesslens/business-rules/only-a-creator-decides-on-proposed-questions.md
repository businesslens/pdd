# Only a Creator decides on proposed questions

Only the Creator of the form a question was proposed for accepts or dismisses
it. Drafting never decides on its own, and a proposed question stays proposed
until the Creator does.

## Rationale

A language model's draft can be wrong; the Creator is the author of every
question Respondents see.
