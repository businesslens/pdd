# Entry question

A question a Respondent answers by entering an answer of their own rather than
picking a listed option: a short answer, a paragraph, a rating, or a date.

## Information kept

- **Prompt** — the question as Respondents read it
- **Answer type** — a short answer, a paragraph, a rating from one to five, or a date
- **Required** — whether a response must answer it whenever it is shown
- **Show condition** — an option of an earlier choice question that must be chosen before this question is shown, if the Creator set one

## States

### Proposed

Drafted by a language model at the Creator's request and waiting beside the
form for the Creator to accept or dismiss it. It has no place in the form and
no Respondent sees it.

### Included

On the form, in its question order, and asked of every Respondent it is shown
to.
