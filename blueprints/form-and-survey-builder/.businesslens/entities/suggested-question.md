# Suggested question

A question the Product drafted for a form at its Creator's request, waiting
beside it for the Creator to decide. It is not part of the form and no Respondent ever sees it.

## Information kept

- **Prompt** — the question as drafted
- **Answer type** — the kind of answer the draft proposes, with any options

## States

### Proposed

Drafted and waiting for the Creator to accept or dismiss it while they work
through the drafts.

### Accepted

Accepted by the Creator, who added it to the form as a question of its own.

### Dismissed

Set aside by the Creator, or left undecided when they finished. It never
became part of the form.
