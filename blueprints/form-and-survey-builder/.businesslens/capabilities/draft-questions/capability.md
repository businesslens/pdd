# Question drafting

When the Creator states what a form should find out and asks for drafts, the Product asks a language model to draft questions from that goal and the questions already on the form, and keeps them as proposed questions beside the form for the Creator to accept or dismiss.

## Intent

Get a Creator from a goal to a first set of questions quickly. The language model is a helper the Product calls only on the Creator's request; without it, building a form by hand is unaffected.
