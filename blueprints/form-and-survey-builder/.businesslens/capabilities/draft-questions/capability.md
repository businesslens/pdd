# Question drafting

Has the Assistant draft suggested questions from what the Creator says the form should find out, and lets the Creator accept the ones they want and dismiss the rest. A suggestion becomes a question only when the Creator accepts it.

## Intent

Get a Creator from a goal to a first set of questions quickly, while every question on the form remains one the Creator chose.
