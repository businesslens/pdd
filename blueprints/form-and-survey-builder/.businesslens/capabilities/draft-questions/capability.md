# Question drafting

When the Creator states what a form should find out and asks for drafts, the Product asks a language model to draft suggested questions from that goal and the questions already on the form, and keeps them as proposals beside the form. The Creator accepts the ones they want and dismisses the rest; a suggestion becomes a question only when the Creator accepts it.

## Intent

Get a Creator from a goal to a first set of questions quickly, while every question on the form remains one the Creator chose. The language model is a helper the Product calls only on the Creator's request; without it, building a form by hand is unaffected.
