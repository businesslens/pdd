# Snippet visibility

Decides who can read an owned snippet: only its owner (private), anyone holding
its address (unlisted), or anyone at all, listed in Discover and open to forking
(public).

## Intent

Give the owner one explicit, reversible control over who can read a snippet,
with the address staying the same whichever they choose.
