# Snippet reading

Presents one snippet — its title, description, owner, language and tags, and
its code highlighted for its language — to whoever may read it, and shows
nothing to anyone who may not.

## Intent

Make the snippet itself the thing that is shared: one address that shows the
code well to everyone its owner allows, and reveals nothing to anyone else.
