# Snippet reading

Presents one snippet — its title, description, owner, language and tags, its
code highlighted for its language, and its revisions newest first — to whoever
may read it, and shows nothing to anyone who may not. An earlier revision opens
with its code, its language and what changed from the revision before.

## Intent

Make the snippet itself the thing that is shared: one address that shows the
code well to everyone its owner allows, reveals nothing to anyone else, and
lets its readers see how it changed.
