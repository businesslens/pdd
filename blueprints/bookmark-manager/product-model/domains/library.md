# Library

The Owner's bookmarks and how they find them again: saving a link, describing
it with a title and note, tagging it, filing it into a collection, browsing and
searching, and deleting it. Collections are opened from the Library rather than
from a section of their own, so creating, renaming and deleting them belong
here too.

## Boundary

Owns bookmarks, the tags they carry and the collections they are filed in. It
does not own importing a browser's bookmarks, or the AI agent's suggestions
about the library.
