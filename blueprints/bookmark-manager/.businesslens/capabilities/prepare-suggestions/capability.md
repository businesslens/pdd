# Suggestion preparation

The assistant reviews bookmarks — the Unsorted ones when the Owner asks, or the
ones an import just added — and prepares pending suggestions: a collection and
tags for bookmarks that belong together, and sets of bookmarks that lead to the
same page. Preparing a suggestion changes nothing in the library.

## Intent

Do the tedious part of tidying a library — reading every link and noticing
what belongs together — while leaving every decision to the Owner.
