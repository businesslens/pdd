# Library browsing

Presents the Owner's bookmarks newest first and narrows them by words they
remember, by collection, by tag, or to the Unsorted ones, then opens a bookmark
or the page it leads to.

## Intent

Let the Owner find a link again from whatever they remember about it, whether
or not they ever filed it.
