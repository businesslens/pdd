# Study session

A block of time for studying one topic: planned ahead in the schedule, or
logged after the Student studied without planning it.

## Information kept

- **Start** — the day and time the session is planned for, or took place
- **Planned minutes** — how long the session is meant to last
- **Logged minutes** — how long the Student actually studied, once logged
- **Notes** — what the Student wrote about the session when logging it

## States

### Planned

In the schedule and not yet logged. A planned session whose day has passed is
shown as missed.

### Logged

The Student recorded the study as done. Its logged minutes count toward the
goal's progress, and no study plan replaces it.
