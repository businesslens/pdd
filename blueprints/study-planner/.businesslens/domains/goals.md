# Goals

What the student is studying toward: each goal and its date, the topics it is
broken into, how far the logged study has brought it, and asking the planner to
plan it.

## Boundary

Owns goals, their topics and topic estimates, the progress derived from
logged study, and the request for a study plan. It does not own when sessions
take place, or the study plans themselves once proposed: their review belongs
to Plans.
