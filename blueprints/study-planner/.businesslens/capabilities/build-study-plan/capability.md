# Study plan building

Builds a study plan for a goal when the Student asks for one: the planner
spreads each topic's study still to do across the Student's weekly
availability before the target date, around the sessions already scheduled for
other goals, and opens the plan for the Student to review.

## Intent

Do the arithmetic of fitting topics into the time the Student has, and say
plainly what does not fit, so the Student starts from a schedule they can keep.
