# Study plan proposals

The Planning assistant prepares a study plan for a goal: it divides the
study still to do across the Student's weekly availability before the target
date, and explains what it did. It prepares one when the Student asks, and a
revised one on its own when a planned session is missed. A plan only proposes;
the schedule changes when the Student accepts it.

## Intent

Do the arithmetic of fitting topics into the time the Student has, and keep doing it as plans meet real weeks, without taking the schedule out of the Student's hands.
