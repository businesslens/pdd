# Card proposals

Leaves proposed cards on one board: cards for a goal a member states, or a next
step for a card the Product has flagged as stalled. The AI agent reads the
board, then suggests cards with a title, a description and a column to start
in; each waits as a proposed card until a member accepts or dismisses it.

## Intent

Take the effort out of breaking a goal into cards, and out of getting stalled
work moving again, while leaving every decision about the board with its
members.
