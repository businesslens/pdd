# Propose cards

Turns a goal a member states into proposed cards for one board. The AI agent
reads the board, then suggests cards with a title, a description and a column
to start in; each waits as a proposed card until a member accepts or dismisses
it.

## Intent

Take the effort out of breaking a goal into cards while leaving every decision
about the board with its members.
