# Stall flag

The Product's mark that a card has stayed in one column longer than its board's
stall threshold, shown on the card to every member of the board.

## Information kept

- **Reason** — which column the card has been in and for how long
- **Raised at** — when the flag was raised

## States

### Raised

Shown on the card until the card moves to another column.

### Cleared

The card moved to another column, so the flag no longer applies and is no
longer shown.
