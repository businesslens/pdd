# Board settings

A board's own settings and its people: its name, the stall threshold its cards
are measured against, who belongs to it, and the role each member holds.

## Boundary

Owns a board's name and stall threshold, its memberships and their roles. It
does not own the board's columns or cards, proposed cards, or the accounts
Teammates sign in with.
