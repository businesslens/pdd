# Stall flags are deleted only with their card

Nobody deletes a stall flag, raised or cleared, on its own. It goes only with
its card, when a member deletes the card or an admin deletes the board.

## Rationale

A flag is part of the card's history on the board; it lasts exactly as long as
the card does.
