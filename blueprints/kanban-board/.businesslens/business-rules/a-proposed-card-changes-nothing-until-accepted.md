# A proposed card changes nothing on the board until a member accepts it

While a card is proposed, the board's columns and cards are exactly as the
members left them. Accepting it adds one card; dismissing it adds nothing.

## Rationale

Members can let proposals wait, or ignore them, without the board drifting away
from what they decided.
