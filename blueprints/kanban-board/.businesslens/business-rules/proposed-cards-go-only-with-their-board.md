# Proposed cards are deleted only with their board

Nobody deletes a proposed card, decided or not, on its own. It goes only with
its board, when an admin deletes the board. A dismissed proposal is kept as
dismissed, not deleted.

## Rationale

The board's proposals record what was suggested to the team and what the team
decided; they last as long as the board does.
