# A decision never changes

Once recorded, a decision's outcome, rationale and final results stay as they
were recorded, for everyone, including its poll's owner.

## Rationale

The team refers back to a decision as what was agreed at the time. Revisiting a
question is a new poll and a new decision, not an edit of the old one.
