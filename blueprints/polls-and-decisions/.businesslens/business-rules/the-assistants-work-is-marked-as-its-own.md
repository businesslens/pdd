# The Assistant's work is marked as its own

Everything the Assistant prepares is shown as the Assistant's, and only to the
poll owner who asked for it. A recorded decision that started from its draft
keeps saying so.

## Rationale

The Assistant chooses what to keep and how to phrase it. The owner, and later
the team, need to know which words a person wrote and which they accepted.
