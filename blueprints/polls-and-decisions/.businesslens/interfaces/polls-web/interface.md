# Polls web application

The supported browser Interface for a team's polls and decisions. Every place
in it is reached by a signed-in Member of the team: putting questions to the
team, voting and discussing, closing polls, and recording and finding
decisions. The Assistant works here too, on a poll owner's request, preparing
what that owner sees on the poll and on its draft decision.
