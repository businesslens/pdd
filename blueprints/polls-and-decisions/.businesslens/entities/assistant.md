# Assistant

The Product's built-in AI assistant. When a poll's owner asks, it reads the
poll and its discussion and prepares words for that owner: a summary of the
arguments, or a draft of the decision. It chooses what to include, which is why
everything it prepares is marked as its own and waits for the owner. It never
votes, comments, closes a poll or records a decision.
