# Polls

The life of a question put to the team: asking it, voting and arguing while it
is open, revealing its results, closing it, and taking it back before anyone
votes.

## Boundary

Owns polls, votes, comments, results, and the comment summary and draft decision
an owner asks for on a poll. It does not own what the team decided once the poll
closed, or the record the team looks back on.
