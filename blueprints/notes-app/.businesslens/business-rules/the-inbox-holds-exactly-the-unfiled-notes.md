# The inbox holds exactly the notes in no notebook

A note is unsorted, and listed in the inbox, exactly when it is filed in no
notebook. Capturing a note puts it there; filing it takes it out; returning it,
or deleting its notebook, puts it back. Editing, tagging, linking and
dismissing a suggestion never move a note in or out of the inbox.

## Rationale

The inbox is only worth emptying if it is a complete and honest list of what is
left to sort.
