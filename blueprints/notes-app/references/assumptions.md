# Notes assumptions

These are assumptions to validate, not claims that research has proved them.

- People capture more notes than they sort, so an inbox of unsorted notes is
  what makes organizing feel finishable.
- One notebook per note, plus any number of tags, is enough structure for a
  personal notes collection.
- Searching by words and narrowing by a tag finds most notes people remember
  only vaguely.
- Owners trust an AI agent with their notes when it can only suggest, every
  suggestion says why, and they can switch the agent off at any time.
- Accepting or dismissing a whole suggestion is quick enough; owners who
  disagree with part of one prefer to file the note themselves.

Future research may change the Product Model; this file does not override it.
