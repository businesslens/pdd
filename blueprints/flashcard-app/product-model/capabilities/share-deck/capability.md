# Deck sharing

Makes an owned deck readable by link: any signed-in Learner holding the share
link sees its cards and may copy it. Sharing never exposes the owner's progress
and never lets anyone else change the deck.

## Intent

Let a Learner hand their cards to classmates or friends without giving up
control of their own deck.
