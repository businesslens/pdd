# Card

One prompt and its answer, studied on its own schedule.

## Information kept

- **Front** — the prompt the Learner tries to answer
- **Back** — the answer revealed after trying
- **Due on** — the day it next comes back for study; a new card is due the day it is added
- **Interval** — the gap its last rating set before it comes back

## States

### New

Added to the deck and never studied.

### Learning

Studied, with a gap still under 21 days, or forgotten and starting over.

### Known

Recalled well enough that its gap has reached 21 days or more.
