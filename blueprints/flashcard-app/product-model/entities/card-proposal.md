# Card proposal

A card the Product drafted from a Learner's pasted notes, waiting in its deck
for the Learner to accept or dismiss. It is never studied.

## Information kept

- **Front** — the drafted prompt
- **Back** — the drafted answer
- **Source passage** — the part of the pasted notes it was drafted from

## States

### Proposed

Drafted and waiting for the Learner's decision.

### Accepted

The Learner accepted it, and its deck holds a new card made from it.

### Dismissed

The Learner turned it down, and nothing was added to its deck.
