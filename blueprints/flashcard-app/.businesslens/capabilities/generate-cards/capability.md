# Generate cards

Takes notes a Learner pastes for an owned deck and has the Assistant draft card
proposals from them. Each proposal keeps the passage it was drafted from; the
pasted notes themselves are not kept. Nothing is added to the deck: proposals
wait for the Learner's decision.

## Intent

Save the Learner the typing while leaving every decision about what to study
with them.
