# Study deck

Works through the cards an owned deck has due, one at a time. The Learner
tries to recall the back from the front, reveals it, and rates their recall
Again, Hard, Good or Easy; the rating decides when the card comes back.

## Intent

Spend study time only on cards that are due, so each session is short and aimed
at what is closest to being forgotten.
