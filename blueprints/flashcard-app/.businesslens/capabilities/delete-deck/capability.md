# Deck deletion

Deletes an owned deck for good, together with its cards, their progress and
its card proposals, once the Learner confirms. Copies other Learners already
made are theirs and stay.

## Intent

Let a Learner clear out a deck they no longer need, after saying plainly what goes with
it.
