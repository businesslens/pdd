Complete placement inventory

There are three reuse contexts for the card, four for the tree, and three presentations within a resource reading. A label appears only when that particular resource belongs to a valid set. Some conditional contexts are not exercised by the current fixture.

Other variation language

Connections and relation graphs may say variation of or name a variation selection reference. These are relationship labels, not type-and-count shortcuts. Conditional Rule popovers show Applies conditionally and the usage fields, with no variation count. Vocabulary has its own Variations group. Search, matrices and graph nodes do not render this type-and-count shortcut.
