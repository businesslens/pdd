ElementMeaning
variationKindDeclared once, on the anchor: experiment, configuration, or version. Other members derive it.
variantOfA direct link to another resource of the same type. It can cross containers. No self-links, chains, cycles, missing targets or implicit defaults.
## When usedRequired on every member, including the anchor. Explains eligibility or selection, defaults/overrides, and stability where relevant. Stored as typed Markdown on the wire and restored on open.
Independent meaningEach member remains complete. Links do not inherit content, pair children, choose a member, or create availability, ownership or permission.

Conditional permissions: applicability is prose. Lint validates the Rule’s structure but warns that selection and permission behavior require verification; it does not treat all Rule alternatives as simultaneously applicable. Conditional prohibitions stay visible through Rule and fact readings and are not drawn as unconditional lifecycle prohibitions. Ordinary unconditional permission checks remain enforced.

These checks do not execute assignments or prove natural-language selection conditions exhaustive. No independent mapping trial establishes deterministic resource granularity or subtype classification for this change.
