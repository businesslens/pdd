02

Authoring

Coverage is not an escape hatch

Before

Skill guidance described some relationship failures as warnings until “complete” coverage, although Coverage has no completeness status.

After

Structural checks apply regardless of Coverage. Known gaps stay in Coverage; they do not relax the contract.

The shipped rubric now matches the validator. This correction does not invent a new completeness field.

skills/businesslens-map/references/format.md · skills/businesslens-ideate/references/format.md · skills/businesslens-verify/references/format.md

03

Product behavior

Separate disclosure from input

Before

One Screen facts list represented both facts the Product shows and facts the Actor supplies. Read-permission checks could interpret an input field as disclosure.

After

Screen entries distinguish shows and collects. A prefilled editable fact can appear in both. Read rules inspect shows; collecting a fact alone is not a read. The report displays separate Presents and Collects sections, grouping each by Entity and omitting empty sections.

A private feed address can be entered without claiming the Product discloses it.

spec/format.md · spec/report.md · src/core/permission-validation.ts · layers/nuxt/report-viewer/app/components/BlrResourceBody.vue

04

Product behavior

Allow prohibitions without examples

Before

A read rule governing a fact no Screen showed and no Step cited was rejected. Permission Contexts also had to contain a disclosure.

After

A permission Rule can prohibit a fact nobody reads. Entity, fact and Context references still resolve, and matching operations remain checked.

“Never disclose the secret” no longer requires a secret disclosure to exist in the model.

src/commands/lint.ts · src/core/portable.ts · docs/business-rules.md

05

Authoring

Use permission conditions only for permissions

Before

Flags and A/B variation were described as grant conditions even when gating a Capability; permits accepts Entity targets only.

After

A grant’s when conditions an Entity operation. Other behavior differences use Scenario conditions/outcomes and cross-behavior Rules. Products that manage experiments may model that behavior with ordinary resources.

Removes an instruction that could not be encoded in the contract.

facts was allowed only on reads and changes, leaving creation facts and defaults outside fact-scoped checks.

07

Product behavior

Make Step fact claims explicit

Before

Optional fact lists could omit affected fields while leaving the Step structurally valid.

After

Reads, changes and creates require facts. The list is exhaustive; [] explicitly means no named facts are affected. Export and expansion preserve that empty-list claim.

Lint catches missing or invalid declarations; verification must still establish whether the declaration is exhaustive against source or approved intent.

08

Authoring · empirical limit

Nest by persistent working context

Before

Opening a view from another view was used as ownership evidence, conflating a launch site with a persistent parent.

After

Nest under the nearest qualifying selected subject or process. Shared destinations live once at their common container. Generic category selectors are not processes; result-only views are Outcomes. The teaching Blueprint’s Source detail is now beside Source list.

Two independent trials were run twice. The revised rule improves some cases but does not establish deterministic Screen identity or containment. See the evidence below.

09

Product behavior

Remove Experience versions

Before

Experience.version and different version values could force an Interface to divide. URL/header distinctions were treated as a shortcut to resource boundaries.

After

The field and its parser, wire, viewer and lint handling are removed. Independently supported contracts may be Interfaces; other variations use Scenarios and Rules. The next-version teaching Experience and its additional mobile behavior are removed. This decision is under renewed review.

Avoids making version labels determine Product structure. No compatibility reader is introduced.

10

Authoring

Apply the normal Capability split test

Before

Filtering, sorting and searching a set were categorically Scenarios and never independent Capabilities.

After

Ordinary browsing refinements remain Scenarios. An independent purpose, permission, availability or outcome can justify a Capability under the general rule.

Stops a browsing shortcut from excluding independently meaningful search behavior.

spec/format.md · docs/capabilities.md · skills/businesslens-ideate/references/planning-rubric.md

11

Authoring

Verify contractual wording

Before

The redesign rule excluded all copy; even required text was pointed to a Reference without a clear exactness check.

After

Ordinary copy stays outside the model. When exact wording is required, a Rule names the authoritative Reference and requires it. Verify compares it or reports that requirement unverifiable if unavailable.

Preserves real Product requirements without introducing a copy resource or UI text catalog.

docs/interfaces.md · skills/businesslens-verify/SKILL.md · skills/businesslens-verify/references/verification-rubric.md

12

Product behavior

Derive Screen Capabilities once

Before

Screens authored capabilities while placed Steps were separately required to support the same list. Reverse coverage required every available Capability to appear on a Screen.

After

Screen Capabilities derive from Steps placed exactly there, across both Scenario kinds. The report validates the exact derived set. A Screen still needs placed behavior; a Capability need not have a Screen. Container-level Steps remain valid beside other Screens.

Removes duplicate authority without forcing background or otherwise non-Screen behavior into a view.
