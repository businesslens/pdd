# Legal and Trust Screens

read_when: changing the privacy policy.

One page with one job: answer the question a visitor actually has, in the words
they would use to ask it. It carries a `Legal` headline and a prose body, and
is generated like every other page.

The security page and the terms were retired with the footer refresh. Nothing
required them: the vulnerability-report path is the `SECURITY.md` policy in
`itai-gendler/pdd-kit`, which the FAQ links, and there is no account tier, payment,
or directly-hosted user content for terms to govern — contributions arrive as
pull requests under GitHub's own terms. Bring terms back before taking money,
hosting user-submitted content outside a pull request, or promising uptime, and
link them from the footer when you do: unlinked terms bind nobody.
